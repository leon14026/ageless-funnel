    Your Program

    What Ageless by Tulee is

    Clinically designed, joint-friendly workouts you can follow from the privacy of your home — no equipment needed, menopause-aware, and safe for your body. A flagship 6-month journey built for your changing body, so you can feel lighter, move freely, and work toward a slimmer, more radiant, younger-looking you — without punishing your joints.

    What you'll accomplish

    💪

    A body that starts responding again through structured movement, realistic nutrition, and routines that meet you where you are.

    ✨

    More confidence for the moments that matter — photos, celebrations, family, work, and the clothes you want to wear again.

    🦵

    Functional strength for real life — stairs, bags, children, travel, and daily movement with less fear and more control.

    🧘

    Mental resilience, renewed energy, and the feeling that you're becoming yourself again — physically, mentally, and emotionally.

    Your 6-month journey

    1. Month 1
      Gentle Reset

      A comfortable foundation of controlled, low-impact movement, mobility warm-ups, and joint prep.

    2. Month 2
      Energy & Glow

      Longer sessions and light resistance for steadier energy, freer movement, and a healthy glow.

    3. Month 3
      Tone & Mobility

      Resistance bands & dumbbells paired with Bangladeshi nutrition tracking for tone and everyday strength.

    4. Month 4
      Slimmer & Lighter

      Variety and progression with portion awareness — a slimmer, lighter feeling, no crash dieting.

    5. Month 5
      Radiance & Resilience

      Habits for balance, posture, calm, and confidence, at a pace that respects your body.

    6. Month 6
      Be Ageless

      A sustainable continuation routine so you keep feeling ageless — physically, mentally, and emotionally.
