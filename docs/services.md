# Services provided on Ageless by Tulee

This is a catalog of what the website currently presents — the paid program, the member platform, free public tools, and items marked coming soon or demo-only. It is based on the live funnel (`index.html`), member app (`/pages/...`), book chapter pages, and the healthy-years calculator. No application code was changed for this document.

**Who it is for:** Bangladeshi women 40+ (and women at any age) who want a private, joint-friendly, menopause-aware wellness journey from home.

**The promise:** Feel lighter. Look brighter. Be Ageless.

## Flagship program: Ageless by Tulee

Ageless by Tulee is a **holistic guided wellness program**. Members follow tailored exercise routines and diet plans, join weekly meetings for encouragement and guidance, and measure progress from home.

Access is sold as a **one-time payment** for 1, 3, or 6 months (not a subscription). The flagship path is a **6-month journey**. Card and international checkout are not offered during pre-order; Bangladesh customers pay by bKash (or bank), and others can join the free waitlist.

## Core services (paid members)

### 1. Clinically designed workouts

Follow-along video workouts, scaled for the menopausal body: joint-safe, progressive, and done from home.

- Six monthly programs; each month is a 7-day routine (6 workout days + 1 rest day) that repeats, then progresses
- Low-impact modifications for every exercise
- Gentle start that builds over 6 months
- No gym required; first weeks are bodyweight; later months offer bands/dumbbells with no-equipment alternatives
- Sessions typically 15–45 minutes
- Available on phone, tablet, and laptop

**Workout styles shown on the site:** yoga, aerobics, toning, strength training, mobility, HIIT, abs, flex band, kickboxing, stretching, healing, balance, chair workouts, partner workouts, meditation, and pranayama.

**The 6-month roadmap**

| Month | Theme | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Gentle Reset | Low-impact movement, mobility, joint prep |
| 2 | Energy & Glow | Longer sessions, light resistance, steadier energy |
| 3 | Tone & Mobility | Bands & dumbbells plus Bangladeshi nutrition tracking |
| 4 | Slimmer & Lighter | Progression and portion awareness, no crash dieting |
| 5 | Radiance & Resilience | Balance, posture, calm, confidence |
| 6 | Be Ageless | A continuation routine you can keep |

On the member dashboard this lives under **Programs** (“This Week’s Workout”). Members can mark days complete and see monthly progress.

### 2. Menopause-aware Bangladeshi nutrition tracking

A food tracker built around everyday Bangladeshi meals rather than Western-only databases.

- 100+ pre-loaded Bangladeshi foods (dal, rice, curry, roti, and similar)
- Calorie and macro goals (protein, carbs, fat) set for the member’s body
- Log breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks
- Weekly trend analysis
- No crash diets and no risky supplementation positioning

On the member dashboard this lives under **Food Log & Nutrition**.

### 3. Custom meal plans

Goal-based plans using natural, whole foods members can customise.

- 50+ pre-built plans by goal and diet type
- Meal swaps and customisation
- Auto-generated grocery lists
- Weekly expert articles and guides (as described on the sales pages)
- Emphasis on practical Bangladeshi food, protein, fiber, and portion awareness

### 4. Progress and accountability tracker

Structured logging so members can see change over time.

- Weight, body fat, and 8+ body measurements
- Weight trend chart
- Progress photos with side-by-side comparison (described on the sales pages)
- Trend charts over 1, 3, and 6 months
- Milestone badges (described on the sales pages)
- Personal nutrition/measurement goals

On the member dashboard this lives under **Progress & Measurements**.

### 5. Coaching, community, and platform

- Personal guidance from **Coach Tulee** (35+ years of coaching)
- Biomedical design input from co-founder **Leon**
- Weekly meetings for encouragement and guidance (described on the homepage)
- Private online member area — no gym commute or gym intimidation
- Member account for profile and preferences
