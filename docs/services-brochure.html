About

Ageless by Tulee is an online wellness program for women aged 40 and above. From home, on any device, members follow guided video workouts, track their nutrition, get custom meal plans, monitor their progress, and receive coaching — all through a private member platform.

What's included

One membership is all-inclusive — a single price unlocks everything below, with no add-ons and nothing billed per feature. Access is a one-time payment (not an auto-renewing subscription), and every membership includes:

  • Guided workout library — follow-along, joint-friendly at-home routines that progress over the program
  • Nutrition tracking — a food log built around everyday Bangladeshi meals, with calorie and macro goals
  • Custom meal plans — goal-based plans using whole foods, with auto-generated grocery lists
  • Progress tracking — weight, body measurements, and trend charts over time
  • Coaching & community — guidance from Coach Tulee and regular online group meetings
  • Member app — a private account for workouts, tracking, and plans
