About the business

Ageless by Tulee is an online (digital) wellness program for women aged 40 and above. Members follow guided at-home video workouts, track their nutrition, receive custom meal plans, monitor their progress, and get coaching — entirely through our web-based member platform. Every service is delivered digitally to the customer's online account; there are no in-person sessions required.

100% digital delivery   After a successful payment, the customer instantly receives login access by email and uses all services online, on any device (phone, tablet, or laptop).

Services offered (paid membership)

Customers purchase time-based access to the Ageless by Tulee program. Access is a one-time payment (not an auto-renewing subscription). Each membership includes all of the following digital services for the chosen term:

  • Guided video workout library — follow-along, joint-friendly at-home routines, progressive over the program
  • Nutrition tracking — a food log built around everyday Bangladeshi meals, with calorie and macro goals
  • Custom meal plans — goal-based plans using natural whole foods, with auto-generated grocery lists
  • Progress tracking — weight, body measurements, and trend charts over time
  • Coaching & community — guidance from Coach Tulee and regular online group meetings
  • Member web app — a private online account for workouts, tracking, and plans

How the service is delivered

All purchases are digital services delivered online. Upon successful payment the customer's account is activated automatically and login details are emailed to them. The customer then signs in at agelessbytulee.com to access their workouts, nutrition tools, meal plans, and progress dashboard for the duration of their chosen term. No goods are shipped and no physical items are involved.
